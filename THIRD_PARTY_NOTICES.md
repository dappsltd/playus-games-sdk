## Cuelume sound definitions

Source: https://github.com/danielwh2/cuelume

Upstream commit: caf15480081da9432a4a982446ce44c9daf3a288

from Cuelume's `src/sounds/{recipes,bubble,mech,press,context}.ts`. Helper-generated
layers have been expanded into literal settings. Playback is implemented by Playus.
